Average Speed Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out average speeds, travel times and the speed a last leg needs from the numbers you type. It assumes each leg is at one steady speed. It does not know speed limits, stops, traffic or conditions. Check those where they apply.

The trip truth

Two legs, each at its own steady speed. Distance and speed can be in any one unit, such as km and km/h.

The time truth

One distance at one speed.

The need truth

The speed the last part needs to reach a target average over the whole distance.

Average Speed Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
